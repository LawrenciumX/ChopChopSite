5 Budget Meals That Taste Expensive

By Ruth Ndoumbi

Published Oct 2025

Ruth and Jean Claude, African immigrants in Canada for 20+ years, celebrate the fusion of their native cuisine with Canadian flavors in our food review channel.

As non-experts but passionate food explorers, we seek diverse tastes, cultural connections, and friendships through shared meals. With joyful levity, we sample dishes, infusing humor into new gastronomic experiences.

Canada's culinary realm, from Quebec's poutine to coastal seafood and Ontario's maple-infused dishes, narrates unique tales. Our goal is to savor and share these flavors, showcasing the cultural heritage behind each dish.

Join our journey exploring Canada's diverse culinary scene as we encapsulate flavors and cultural depth in each meal through our videos, fostering an appreciation for diverse culinary identities.
